Exness pros and cons

Pros

  • Four pricing choices

    Standard/Pro: no commission. Zero/Raw Spread: per-side commission.

  • 24/7 payment requests

    Bank settlement follows broker processing; timing varies.

  • Browser and mobile

    Documented platforms; compatibility varies by account.

  • Virtual-money demo

    Practice before funding; demo execution was not tested.

  • No management fee advertised

    Opening/management are free; third-party payment charges may apply.

Cons & checks

  • Spread floors are not typical costs

    Zero-spread headlines can include commission; actual spreads vary with liquidity.

  • Bank settlement can take longer

    Automatic processing does not guarantee immediate receipt; payment-provider delays can apply.

  • Group licences are not universal protection

    UK/Cyprus entities do not serve retail clients; check your contracting company.

  • Higher initial funding for professional accounts

    Pro, Zero and Raw Spread start at US$200, depending on region. Payment-method minimums also apply.

  • High leverage increases loss exposure

    Eligibility and instrument/account limits apply; higher leverage amplifies losses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Exness a market maker?

Execution role remains unverified; consult your entity’s agreement.

Which Exness company would hold my account?

Your registration agreement names the company. Location and account terms determine allocation; UK/Cyprus entities do not serve retail clients.

Is Exness available in every country?

No. Restrictions include the USA. Confirm your location’s eligibility and assigned company during registration.

Can I withdraw money from Exness on weekends?

Requests are available 24/7. Automatic processing does not guarantee receipt; non-instant requests are described as processed within 24 hours, with receiving-provider time additional.

Does Exness charge an inactivity fee?

Exness states no inactivity or reactivation fee. Inactive real accounts may be archived and need reactivation before trading.
