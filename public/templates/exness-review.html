Is Exness safe? Regulation and security

A licence applies to its named legal entity. It does not guarantee returns, account eligibility or protection against trading losses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Exness a market maker?

This draft has not verified the contractual execution role of Exness (KE) Limited. Check that entity’s customer agreement and execution policy; another group company’s model should not be assumed to apply.

Which legal entity would hold my account?

The local website identifies Exness (KE) Limited. CMA lists Exness KE Limited under licence 162. The local offering is restricted to Kenyan residents and Kenyan-incorporated legal entities; confirm the name in your actual agreement.

Which protections apply in my country?

The CMA register confirms the named Kenyan licence holder. This check does not establish a compensation entitlement, negative balance protection or the protections of an overseas group entity.

Can I withdraw money from Exness on weekends?

The Kenyan payment page says deposits and withdrawals can be requested 24/7. “Instant” describes automatic processing, not guaranteed arrival. Non-instant requests are described as processed within 24 hours; banks and payment providers can take longer. Check the selected method in your Personal Area.
