## Source treatment

The [source ledger](country-guide-source-checks-2026-11-07.json) separates documents retrieved directly from official quotations supplied by the owner’s research agent. Automated access failed for some RBI, BOU, BOZ and RBZ pages; those are not falsely recorded as independently retrieved. Country-specific transfer permission remains unresolved for six jurisdictions. This is not a finding that the activity is prohibited.

India describes the specific LRS margin-remittance exclusion and authorised-person/purpose/venue requirements; it does not turn a general remittance limit into permission to fund CFDs. Zimbabwe’s general investment allowance is not presented as a CFD deposit entitlement. The older Zambia SI No.55 reporting reference is not used to establish current permission.

No broker rankings, tested withdrawals, universal payment support, live spreads, deposit minimums, fixed tax rates, professional author credentials or profitable outcomes were invented. Examples are clearly labelled arithmetic.
