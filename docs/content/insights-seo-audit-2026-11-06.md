## Editorial decisions

The SEO title starts with the exact focus phrase and uses a truthful benefit word. Branding is added only when the full title remains within 65 characters. Character limits are an editorial convention, not a guarantee about search-result display.

Each guide includes visible contextual anchors, at least three distinct authoritative external sources, licensed local WebP images, readable subheadings, a contents menu and three brief matching FAQ answers. Short lists, comparison tables and labelled examples make the longer explanation easier to scan.

Density is reported honestly as a diagnostic. We did not force an exact long-tail phrase into 1–1.5% of the copy; natural variations and clear explanations support the topic without stuffing. No Google ranking or Rank Math score is claimed. The four proposed 2,500-word expansions were not applied after the user confirmed the shorter limit. Summary tables and brief FAQs further reduce the time needed to find an answer.
