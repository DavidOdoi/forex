# Prop firm pages: source and publication record

Prepared 9 November 2026 for the two new root routes. The owner’s uploaded brief explicitly requests creation and publication. Existing review drafts remain unchanged and excluded from the public sitemap.

## Editorial scope

The comparison uses five programme-specific, officially documented EA permissions. No accounts, EAs, execution or payouts were personally tested. “Best for” means a compatibility use case, not a financial-strength rating or measured performance. No Trustpilot stars, invented regulatory status or affiliate relationships were added. Evaluation balances are not deposits or a promise of live capital. Region and account eligibility require the provider’s current agreement.

The specified natural-language title/H1 are used instead of forcing the grammatically awkward keyword “best prop firm forex eas” into every heading. This follows the same brief’s instruction against keyword stuffing.

## Facts deliberately not asserted

- FundingPips could not be read through the research fetcher (access denied), so it is omitted rather than assigned invented rules.
- No all-in EA add-on fee was verified for FundedNext; the base price is explicitly labelled and readers are directed to checkout for the complete total.
- No universal grid/martingale or scalping approval, worldwide availability, blanket third-party-bot approval or common “funded” account structure is inferred.
- No firm-specific consistency presets are enabled. The calculator covers **best profitable day / total net profit**, not other denominators, positive-day-only calculations, equity-based calculations or strict “below” boundaries.

## Calculator numerical contract

Inputs accept positive USD amounts and limits to two decimals, up to $1bn and 100%. Invalid/blank/zero/negative values do not produce a usable ratio. A best day greater than net profit is valid after losses. Money and percentage inputs become exact integer cents/basis points; integer cross-multiplication determines inclusive status. Required total rounds **up** to a cent. Additional profit assumes the highest profitable day remains unchanged. Example $2,400 / $1,500 / 40% produces 62.5%, $3,750 required and $1,350 additional. A rounded displayed percentage never overrides the exact boundary.

## Maintenance

Recheck EA policies, platform eligibility and base fees monthly and before any material edit. Update the visible verification date only after reviewing sources again. Recheck immediately after provider rule announcements; remove a provider if its policy cannot be supported. The calculator is independent of named firms: denominator, period and inclusive boundary must match the user’s agreement. FAQ schema mirrors visible answers and does not imply eligibility for a Google rich result. New pages follow existing noindex behaviour on preview deployments and indexability on the confirmed production domain.
