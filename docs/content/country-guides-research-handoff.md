Prepared 7 November 2026. The owner requested nine country-specific guides using the published South Africa guide’s format, with no invented facts. Kenya and Australia have sufficient retrieved primary material for their core beginner/verification paths. Research received later on 7 November clarified the publication scope: all nine guides can explain education and verification checks. The questions below are retained for future product-specific research, rather than as prerequisites to publishing the narrower educational copy. See the [source ledger](country-guide-source-checks-2026-11-07.json) for independently retrieved and owner-supplied evidence.

## Required response format

For every finding provide: country; exact claim; official source URL; source title and issuing institution; publication/effective date; a short relevant quotation; which activity it covers; important exceptions; and whether the source is current or superseded. If the source does not establish permission, say so.

Distinguish ordinary currency conversion, licensed forex bureaux, bank remittances, exchange-traded currency derivatives, OTC leveraged forex/CFDs, broker solicitation, advising others and managing client money. A general company registration, foreign licence, website access or payment acceptance does not establish local product permission. Do not substitute another AI’s confidence or a broker’s blog for primary evidence.

## Access limitations observed

Several primary sites or robots files could not be retrieved reliably in this execution environment. This is a research-access limitation, not proof that the activity is legal, illegal, regulated or unregulated. India’s RBI guidance, Bank of Uganda, Bank of Zambia, SEC Zambia, RBZ and SEC Ghana need additional primary-source access. Some public regulator registers were retrieved but do not, by themselves, establish offshore margin-funding permission.

Do not invent country-specific leverage limits, minimum deposits, accepted payment wallets, bank transfer limits, withdrawal times, investor compensation, regulator approval, tax rates or broker recommendations. Supply primary evidence for any such proposed addition. The copy can explain general training and risk management without those claims.
