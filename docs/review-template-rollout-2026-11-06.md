# Review template rollout — 6 November 2026

The improved Exness opening is now the shared format for all 325 CSV broker/provider identities and their 344 global/country profiles. The template demonstration is checked as a 345th route.

## Applied across the directory

- Two short introductory paragraphs, beginning with the actual broker/provider focus phrase, with contextual links to account/access requirements, fees and safety.
- A Quick answer with a bold first sentence and short safety/withdrawal or access/settlement checks. It reflects the evidence available to the individual profile.
- Retail profiles retain five account actions and now share the adjustable hypothetical EUR/USD cost calculator. Its caption names the actual broker and does not imply that the default costs were observed there.
- Institutional, interbank and introducing profiles retain their research classification and access/settlement framing; retail onboarding, instruments and account calculators are not inferred.
- Three brief FAQs per profile, rendered from the same records as their FAQPage markup. Source-backed answers require valid evidence. Explicit research-status answers report the profile’s known evidence gaps; they do not invent financial terms or attach unsupported citations.
- Primary-topic wording at the start of descriptions and in the overview H2; concise title/description variants for long names and countries; stable scoped canonicals and existing related-profile links.
- The same white background, black 18px body text, mobile account cards, accessible contents, risk/commercial disclosures and factual-evidence guards.

## Relevant product imagery

Five broker-specific image records are available: Exness, eToro, Pepperstone, Afterprime and ADSS. The four newly acquired previews come from the providers’ own pages and have visible source credits. The registry restricts each image to its broker and declared editorial scopes. Pepperstone’s preview explicitly identifies Australia. Small originals are not enlarged beyond their natural width. Generic stock images, decorative abstracts and platform-logo badges were not used as substitute broker screenshots.

The image appears beside the Quick answer on desktop and immediately after it on mobile. Intrinsic dimensions reserve its space; the small local WebP loads eagerly in this opening position. These are provider product illustrations, not funded-account tests or proof of regional availability.

## Verification and remaining limits

Production build, TypeScript and ESLint passed. All 68 Playwright checks passed, covering every broker/scope’s intro and three FAQs, source integrity, broker/scope image matching, no-JavaScript content, mobile layouts, shared navigation, five retail account actions and calculator interactions. This rollout improves presentation and navigation. It does not complete missing broker research, create affiliate destinations, assign customer/editorial ratings, independently verify all licences, or record editorial approval. Evidence gaps remain visible. Unapproved review drafts remain noindex and excluded from the published-review sitemap. Ranking and factual-accuracy percentages are not asserted.
