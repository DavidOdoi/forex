## XM fees and spreads

XM offers several account structures with different trading costs.

The three main forex pricing models are Standard, Ultra Low, and Zero.

Not every account is available under every XM entity.

### Standard Account: No separate commission

The Standard Account charges forex trading costs primarily through the spread.

According to [ForexBrokers.com's independent pricing analysis](https://www.forexbrokers.com/reviews/xm), its tested Standard Account averaged approximately **2.0 pips on EUR/USD**.

The minimum spread was approximately 1.6 pips.

This makes the Standard Account relatively expensive compared with many low-cost forex alternatives.

Its main appeal is simple pricing and a low initial deposit requirement.

### Ultra Low Account: Tighter spreads

The Ultra Low Account also uses spread-based pricing without a separate commission on the tested forex trades.

However, its quoted spreads are generally tighter.

ForexBrokers.com's 2026 review reported:

- Average EUR/USD spread: approximately 1.1 pips.
- Minimum EUR/USD spread: approximately 0.8 pips.
- Separate forex commission: none on the tested account.

For traders who prefer spread-only pricing, the Ultra Low Account appears more cost-effective than the Standard Account under those tested conditions.

### Zero Account: Raw spreads plus commission

The Zero Account combines lower quoted spreads with a separate dealing commission.

The tested commission was:

- $3.50 per side per standard lot.
- $7 for a complete round-turn trade.

When the raw spread is zero, the commission alone represents approximately 0.7 pips for a standard EUR/USD position.

Real trading costs will usually be higher whenever a positive spread applies.
