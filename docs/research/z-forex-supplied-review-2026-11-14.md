# ZForex Review 2026: Is It Safe or a Scam? Regulation, Fees & Withdrawals

**ZForex is an offshore forex and CFD broker offering high leverage, low minimum deposits, and access to MetaTrader 5 and cTrader. However, serious questions about its regulatory authorization make it a high-risk choice for traders.**

Operating through zforex.com, the broker identifies Z Forex Capital Market LLC as its operating company. The company is registered in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and advertises a brokerage licence from the Mwali International Services Authority (MISA).

ZForex offers several trading accounts, including Standard, ECN, and Swap-Free, with minimum deposits starting at $10. It also advertises leverage up to 1:1000 and trading across forex, stock CFDs, indices, metals, and commodities.

These trading conditions may appear attractive, particularly to traders looking for low entry costs and automated trading platforms.

However, an important issue changes the assessment.

**The Central Bank of the Comoros does not recognize MISA as an authorized financial regulator.** That creates serious questions about the protection available to customers relying on ZForex's advertised licence.

Customer feedback raises additional concerns. Some traders praise the platforms and payment processing, while others report account closures, disputed profits, and withdrawal difficulties.

This ZForex review examines its regulatory status, account types, fees, trading platforms, customer complaints, withdrawal conditions, and alternatives.

**Our verdict: ZForex is a high-risk broker that we do not recommend.** Its advertised trading conditions do not outweigh the uncertainty surrounding its regulatory authorization and customer protections.

**Risk warning:** Leveraged forex and CFD trading can result in substantial financial losses. Trading with a provider without recognized financial supervision can introduce additional custody and dispute-resolution risks.

## Is ZForex a good forex broker?

## Is ZForex regulated?

ZForex states that Z Forex Capital Market LLC is registered in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines under registration number 2145 LLC 2022.

It also claims authorization from MISA under licence T2023321.

The [official ZForex regulatory page](https://zforex.com/about-us/regulation-and-license/) describes the licence as authorizing international brokerage and clearing-house activities.

However, this claim must be evaluated against official regulatory information.

### The Comoros central bank warning

On December 10, 2025, the Comoros Ministry of Finance published a warning from the country's central bank.

The warning identified MISA among organizations purporting to issue financial licences without recognized authority.

The central bank stated that licensing banks and financial institutions falls within its jurisdiction, and that the named organizations lacked the necessary legal standing.

Read the [official Comoros Ministry of Finance publication](https://finances.gouv.km/actualite_post/communique-de-la-banque-centrale-des-comores-sur-lexercice-illegal-dactivites-bancaires-offshores/).

A separate [Ministry of Finance clarification](https://finances.gouv.km/actualite_post/note-dinformation-suite-au-communique-de-la-banque-centrale-des-comores/) also discusses unauthorized licensing activity involving forex and cryptocurrencies.

### What does this mean for ZForex?

ZForex claims to hold a MISA certificate.

However, **a certificate issued by an organization whose licensing authority is rejected by the country's central bank should not be treated as equivalent to recognized financial regulation**.

We could not verify a separate forex brokerage authorization for ZForex from the UK's FCA, Australia's ASIC, Cyprus's CySEC, or another established financial-services regulator.

This does not by itself establish that ZForex has committed fraud.

It does mean that traders should not assume the company operates under the same regulatory protections as brokers licensed by those authorities.

### Is ZForex regulated in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines?

No forex brokerage licence from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines was established.

The country's Financial Services Authority states that it does not license forex brokerage activities.

Its [official warning about unlicensed forex operations](https://svgfsa.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/UNLICENSED-FOREX.pdf) explains that registering a company in the jurisdiction does not amount to a forex trading licence.

ZForex's SVG company registration should therefore be described as corporate registration, not a financial-services authorization.

## ZForex leverage: Is 1:1000 safe?

ZForex advertises maximum leverage of 1:1000 for Standard and ECN accounts.

This creates significant risk.

For example, at 1:1000 leverage, a $100,000 notional forex position could theoretically require only $100 of initial margin, before additional account-specific requirements.

A relatively small adverse price movement can eliminate the posted margin.

High leverage is not inherently evidence of a better broker.

It increases the importance of execution quality, margin rules, stop-out policies, and customer protections.

**Our recommendation:** Do not choose ZForex simply because it advertises higher leverage than established regulated alternatives.
