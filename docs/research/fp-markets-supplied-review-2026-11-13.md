## FP Markets fees and spreads

Trading costs are among FP Markets' biggest selling points.

The broker offers two main forex pricing models: Standard and Raw.

### Standard Account: No separate commission

The Standard Account uses spread-based pricing.

According to the broker's published [forex spreads and commission information](https://www.fpmarkets.com/en-ug/forex-spreads/), Standard Account spreads are advertised from approximately 1.0 pip, with no separate forex dealing commission.

The broker's charge is incorporated into the bid-ask spread.

This structure can be easier to understand for beginners and less frequent traders.

### Raw Account: Tight spreads plus commission

The Raw Account advertises spreads starting from 0.0 pips.

For USD-denominated accounts, FP Markets generally publishes a commission of:

- $3 per side per standard lot.
- $6 for a complete round-turn trade.
- Proportionally smaller amounts for smaller trade sizes.

Commission schedules can differ by account currency, platform, and legal entity.

The published [FP Markets pricing details](https://www.fpmarkets.com/en-ug/forex-spreads/) should be checked before assuming a rate applies to a particular account.
