## Does Interactive Brokers offer SIPC protection?

Eligible securities accounts held at **Interactive Brokers LLC** are protected by the Securities Investor Protection Corporation.

According to [IBKR's official client-protection page](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/general/security-investor-protection.php), standard SIPC protection provides:

- Up to $500,000 per eligible account.
- A cash sublimit of $250,000 within that coverage.

Interactive Brokers LLC also maintains excess SIPC insurance through certain Lloyd's of London underwriters.

The additional policy offers up to $30 million per eligible account, including a cash sublimit of $900,000, subject to an aggregate limit.

### What does SIPC actually protect?

SIPC protection is designed to address certain missing customer securities and cash if a member broker-dealer fails.

It does **not** protect investors against:

- Falling stock prices
- Poor trading decisions
- Normal forex losses
- Market volatility
- Futures and options-on-futures losses
- Every type of financial dispute

### Does SIPC cover every international IBKR account?

No.

SIPC protection applies according to the legal entity, account structure, assets, and applicable law.

An account with Interactive Brokers Ireland or another foreign company should not automatically be described as receiving the same protections as an account held by Interactive Brokers LLC.

**Our safety assessment:** Interactive Brokers has a strong regulatory presence, substantial operating history, and public financial disclosures. However, investor protection must always be evaluated at the legal-entity and product level.
