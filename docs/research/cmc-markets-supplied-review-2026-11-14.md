**Our verdict:** CMC Markets is particularly attractive to experienced forex and CFD traders who prioritize market depth, advanced charting, and regulatory oversight. ## CMC Markets fees and spreads

CMC Markets offers two primary forex pricing arrangements:

- Standard, which uses spread-based pricing.
- FX Active, which uses tighter spreads with a separate commission.

Understanding the difference is essential before comparing trading costs.

### Standard Account: Spread-only forex pricing

The Standard Account generally includes the broker's forex dealing charge within the bid-ask spread.

According to [ForexBrokers.com's October 2026 pricing analysis](https://www.forexbrokers.com/reviews/cmc-markets), the average EUR/USD spread during its testing was approximately **1.3 pips**.

This is higher than the advertised minimum spread.

CMC's [official forex product page](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en/forex) publishes minimum spreads that vary by currency pair and market conditions.

A minimum spread should not be interpreted as the average price a trader will receive.

### FX Active Account: Raw-style spreads plus commission

The [official CMC Markets FX Active page](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en/forex/forex-active-trader) advertises spreads starting from 0.0 pips on six major forex pairs.

The commission is:

- 0.0025% of transaction notional value per side.
- $2.50 per side for a $100,000 notional transaction.
- $5 for opening and closing that $100,000 position.

For other forex pairs, CMC advertises a 25% spread discount compared with its standard pricing, subject to the published conditions.

The commission should be considered alongside the actual spread.

### Does CMC Markets charge an inactivity fee?

Yes, in certain jurisdictions.

Under [CMC Markets' UK inactivity policy](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en-gb/support/faqs/charges), a dormant trading account may incur a **£10 monthly fee** after one continuous year without open positions or trading activity.

The fee is deducted only while eligible funds remain.

The UK policy also allows certain recent inactivity charges to be refunded when trading resumes.

Other jurisdictions use different fee amounts and currencies.

For example, CMC's [Singapore policy](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en-sg/support/faqs/charges) lists SGD 15 or USD 15 monthly after 12 months of inactivity.

### Does CMC Markets charge overnight financing?

Yes.

Leveraged positions held beyond the applicable daily cutoff can incur holding costs.

The amount depends on the instrument, position direction, relevant financing rates, and account terms.

CMC explains its financing policies on its [trading-cost page](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en/trading-costs).

### Are share CFDs commission-free?

Not necessarily.

Although standard forex trading is generally spread-based, trading CFDs on shares may involve separate commissions.

CMC may also charge for certain market-data subscriptions.

These costs are explained in its [share-CFD charges guidance](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en-ie/support/faqs/charges).

## CMC Markets guaranteed stop-loss orders

CMC Markets offers guaranteed stop-loss orders on eligible instruments.

A guaranteed stop aims to close a position at the specified stop price even when the market gaps through that level.

However, this protection involves a premium.

According to the [official guaranteed stop-loss explanation](https://www.cmcmarkets.com/en/trading-platforms/guaranteed-stop-loss-orders), the premium is refunded in full if the guaranteed stop is not triggered.

### How is a guaranteed stop different from a regular stop?

A standard stop-loss order can experience slippage during rapid price movements.

A guaranteed stop provides a contractual exit-price guarantee under its terms.

However, it does not eliminate investment losses, and traders must still choose an appropriate stop level and understand the cost.
