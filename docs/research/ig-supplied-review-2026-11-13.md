## IG fees and spreads

IG generally uses spread-based pricing for standard retail forex CFD trading.

Instead of charging a separate commission on each standard forex CFD trade, the broker incorporates its dealing charge into the quoted bid-ask spread.

However, spreads vary according to market conditions, account type, trading instrument, and jurisdiction.

### IG EUR/USD spread

IG advertises a minimum EUR/USD spread of approximately **0.6 pips** for certain retail forex products.

The minimum is the tightest advertised spread under favorable conditions.

It is not a guarantee that traders will consistently receive that price.

ForexBrokers.com's August 2026 review reported an average EUR/USD spread of approximately **0.91 pips**, based on its October 2025 testing sample.

That makes the average more useful for practical comparison than the advertised minimum alone.

These examples come from IG's published forex product specifications. They are minimum spreads, not live or guaranteed execution prices.

### How much would an EUR/USD trade cost?

For an illustrative one-standard-lot EUR/USD position, one pip is generally worth approximately $10 when the account's profit and loss are measured in USD.

At a 0.6-pip spread, the spread cost would be approximately $6.

At a 0.91-pip spread, the cost would be approximately $9.10.

These calculations exclude overnight funding, slippage, currency-conversion charges, and any product-specific fees.

The actual cost of a trade can differ significantly.
