## Tickmill fees and spreads

Trading costs are Tickmill's primary selling point.

The broker offers two main retail forex pricing structures: Classic and Raw.

It also offers a separate TradingView Raw arrangement.

### Classic Account: Commission-free trading

The Classic Account uses spread-based pricing.

There is no separate forex dealing commission.

Instead, Tickmill incorporates its trading charge into the bid-ask spread.

According to [ForexBrokers.com's May 2026 pricing analysis](https://www.forexbrokers.com/reviews/tickmill), the Classic Account averaged approximately **1.7 pips on EUR/USD** during its testing.

That is relatively expensive compared with several low-cost spread-only and raw-spread competitors.

### Raw Account: Tight spreads plus commission

The Raw Account offers spreads starting from 0.0 pips.

According to [Tickmill's official account comparison](https://www.tickmill.com/trading/accounts-overview), its commission is:

- $3 per side per standard lot.
- $6 for a complete round-turn transaction.
- Proportionally smaller commissions for smaller positions.

This pricing structure can be attractive to active traders who want to reduce transaction costs.

### TradingView Raw Account

Tickmill offers a separate TradingView Raw account with direct TradingView integration.

Its official pricing is:

- Spreads from 0.0 pips.
- $3.50 per side per standard lot.
- $7 round-turn commission.

This account costs $1 more per round-turn standard lot in commission than the ordinary Raw Account.

The additional cost should be considered alongside the benefits of TradingView integration.
