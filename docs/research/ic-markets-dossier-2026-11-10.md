# IC Markets research dossier for DEVIN FOREX

Research date: **10 November 2026**.  
Scope: global review, separating Seychelles, Cyprus, Australia, Bahamas and the Cayman Islands where evidence was found.

The most important finding is that a single worldwide IC Markets specification would be misleading. Current official pages advertise no minimum deposit and dynamic leverage up to 1:5000 for certain Seychelles accounts, whereas Australia advertises a US$200 starting deposit and 1:30 retail leverage, and Cyprus states a €200-equivalent minimum and up to 1:30 retail leverage. [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.com/global/en/trading-accounts/overview)

This dossier contains verified findings and clearly identified gaps. It is not a claim that every entity’s complete legal and payment documentation has been verified.

### Raw Pro, Raw Pro+ and Starter: do not treat as confirmed generally available

The Seychelles overview advertises:

- Raw Pro: US$5,000 deposit, $1.50 per lot/side.
- Raw Pro+: US$100,000 deposit, $1 per lot/side.
- Both show 0.0 minimum spreads and “Get early access.” [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.com/global/en/trading-accounts/overview)

The dedicated page explicitly says customers can trade existing Raw Spread/Standard accounts “when Raw Pro, Raw Pro+ and Starter are ready.” It also shows 1:1000, conflicting with 1:5000 on the overview. Therefore, classify these as early-access/announced offerings pending confirmation—not standard live options. Starter specifications were not established. [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.com/global/en/forex-trading-platform-metatrader/raw-pro)

## Three useful FAQs

### Is IC Markets’ minimum deposit $200?

Not universally. Current Global/Seychelles pages say no minimum for ordinary accounts. Australia advertises US$200 equivalent; Cyprus says €200 equivalent. Actual funding-method limits and margin requirements can still apply. [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.com/global/en/trading-accounts/overview)

### Does IC Markets protect everyone against negative balances?

No universal protection was established. Australia and Cyprus document retail NBP; Cyprus professional clients are excluded. Global/Seychelles help warns customers they bear the consequences of negative equity. [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.com/global/en/help-resources/help-centre)

### Is an IC Markets swap-free account free to hold overnight?

No. Global swap-free accounts replace swaps with a holding-fee structure after applicable grace periods, with some instruments charged from day one. Cyprus currently says it does not offer Islamic accounts. [icmarkets](https://www.icmarkets.eu/en/help-resources/faqs)
