# Broker review additions — 14 November 2026

Eight supplied articles were archived unchanged alongside this note and adapted into the existing shared review template: XTB, Interactive Brokers, AvaTrade, BlackBull Markets, eToro, AAFX Trading, ZForex and Trading.com.

## Evidence and editorial treatment

- XTB: read the official UK account/fee schedule, help-centre conversion/inactivity policy and group disclosures. UK prices are labelled UK examples. The current UK withdrawal page differs from older threshold-fee summaries. Both inactivity conditions (365 days without opening/closing a position AND 90 days without a deposit) are preserved. The application route redirected to an empty mobile gateway during checks. The review therefore uses the working official international homepage as the account-entry gateway; completion was not tested.
- Interactive Brokers: read the spot-currency commission table, ordinary account minimums, platform descriptions, investor-protection page, spot-currency product page and withdrawal FAQ. The current FAQ permits two free withdrawal requests/month. First-tier manual FX commission is 0.20 basis points of notional, with US$2 minimum per order, not a fixed per-lot or round-trip fee. The review calculator respects that minimum and states its equal opening/closing-rate assumption. Brokerage access is not represented as automatic leveraged-FX eligibility.
- AvaTrade: read current fees, minimum-deposit FAQ, regulatory disclosures, AvaProtect terms and withdrawal instructions. Both quarterly inactivity and separate annual administration fees are retained with legal/currency qualifications. Optional protection has a premium and limited scope; processing estimates are not receipt guarantees.
- BlackBull: read the main ECN comparison, account-type explanation, deposit/withdrawal charges, compliance disclosures and platform page. The source also contains a differently named account table with different minimums, so the discrepancy is disclosed. The main ECN table lists US$0 minimums and US$3/lot/side on Prime/Prime+. The US$5 withdrawal charge is retained. New Zealand FSPR registration is not presented as proof of a derivatives licence.
- eToro: read current fees, deposits, CopyTrader and entity disclosures. The current fee page lists free investment-account inactivity, standard-tier crypto fees on buying and selling, and US$5/USD-account versus eligible free local-currency withdrawals. USDC, tier, country, conversion and product exceptions are retained; no single global first-deposit amount is asserted.
- AAFX: read the CFTC RED List entry and Hong Kong SFC unlicensed-entity alert directly. The account page returned a security-verification screen, including in a normal browser; no access challenge was bypassed. Promotional account figures therefore remain explicitly supplied research, with separate provenance and evidence status. The November dossier’s intake date is 14 November, not an invented precise research date. CFTC inclusion is not described as a court finding of fraud. Unverified individual complaint allegations and the separate Spanish warning were not reproduced as independently checked facts.
- ZForex: read the Comoros Ministry of Finance publication of the central-bank warning, the broker’s MISA claim, account table and payment policy. The authority’s general warning is not described as a broker-specific fraud judgment. Current account terms distinguish US$3.50 forex commission per side from US$5 metals commission per side; the supplied review’s ambiguous commission descriptions were corrected. Only the platform actually documented in the checked source is stated; no unsupported cTrader or Trustpilot-restriction claim is made.
- Trading.com: read the US, UK, EU and Australian account pages, regional regulatory disclosures and MT5 page. US forex is separated from international CFDs. The regional 0.6-pip headline is not asserted as an independently measured EUR/USD spread. No unverified universal dormancy amount, withdrawal fee or timing is invented. The account link leads to the official US T1 application entry; readers must confirm residence and the correct regional company.

No live account was opened/funded, no execution or withdrawal performance was tested, and no Trustpilot score/count or performance rating was fabricated. Provider-reported licences are labelled accordingly; independent register confirmation and editorial approval remain outstanding.
