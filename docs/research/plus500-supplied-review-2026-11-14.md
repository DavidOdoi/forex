## Plus500 fees and spreads

Plus500's main CFD account uses spread-based pricing.

The broker generally does not charge a separate commission when opening or closing standard CFD positions.

Instead, trading costs are incorporated into the difference between the buying and selling prices.

The broker explains this model on its [official fees and charges page](https://www.plus500.com/en/help/feescharges).

Plus500's forex spreads vary depending on the instrument and market conditions.

## Plus500 guaranteed stop-loss orders

Guaranteed stop orders are a particularly useful feature for understanding Plus500's risk-management tools.

An ordinary stop-loss order may execute at a worse price during rapid market movements or price gaps.

A guaranteed stop aims to close an eligible position at the specified stop price even when the market moves through that level, subject to the order's terms.

However, guaranteed stops involve an additional cost.

According to [Plus500's official guaranteed-stop explanation](https://cdn-main.plus500.com/en/tradingacademy/faq/trading/whatisguaranteedstoporder), the cost is incorporated through a wider spread.

### What are the limitations?

- Not available on every instrument.
- Must be placed when opening an eligible position.
- Subject to minimum and maximum stop distances.
- Cannot simply be treated as a free standard stop.
- Additional spread cost is non-refundable after activation under the published conditions.

A guaranteed stop can control a particular exit-price risk, but it does not prevent losses or make leveraged trading safe.
