## eToro CopyTrader review

CopyTrader is eToro's signature feature.

It allows eligible users to automatically replicate selected investors' trades and portfolio allocations.

Instead of buying and selling every asset manually, a user allocates funds to an investor they want to copy.

The platform then aims to mirror that investor's portfolio proportionally.

Learn more through the [official eToro CopyTrader page](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/).

### How does CopyTrader work?

A user can browse available investors, review their performance history, assess risk information, and choose an allocation.

The platform then copies eligible trades according to the selected settings.

Users may also stop copying, pause a copy arrangement, or adjust certain risk controls.

### What is the minimum amount for CopyTrader?

According to [eToro's official CopyTrader guidance](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/), the minimum amount required to copy one trader is **$200**.

Minimum position sizes also apply.

Account and product restrictions can affect which underlying positions are copied successfully.

### Does CopyTrader charge additional fees?

eToro states that copying another investor does not itself trigger a separate CopyTrader subscription charge.

However, ordinary trading fees still apply.

These can include cryptocurrency transaction charges, forex spreads, and other applicable costs.

### Can you lose money with CopyTrader?

Yes.

CopyTrader is not a guaranteed investment strategy.

A copied investor can make poor decisions, hold concentrated positions, use leverage, or experience substantial drawdowns.

The copied portfolio can also perform differently because of transaction timing, minimum order sizes, and market conditions.

### How should you evaluate someone to copy?

Consider a potential copied investor's:

- Long-term performance rather than short-term gains
- Maximum historical drawdown
- Risk score
- Asset allocation
- Use of leverage
- Portfolio concentration
- Trading frequency
- Length of verified trading history

Historical performance is not a reliable predictor of future returns.

**Our CopyTrader assessment:** eToro offers one of the most accessible social trading experiences, but users should treat copied positions as real investments carrying real financial risk.
