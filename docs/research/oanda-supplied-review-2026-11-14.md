## OANDA fees and spreads

Trading fees are one of OANDA's main weaknesses compared with low-cost forex brokers.

OANDA offers different pricing structures depending on the jurisdiction and account.

### Standard Account: Spread-only pricing

On eligible Standard Accounts, OANDA generally incorporates the trading charge into the bid-ask spread.

There is no separate forex commission under this model.

According to [ForexBrokers.com's May 2026 pricing analysis](https://www.forexbrokers.com/reviews/oanda), OANDA's EUR/USD spread averaged approximately **1.69 pips during August 2025**.

The analysis included rollover periods when spreads tend to widen.

Excluding rollover periods, the reported average was approximately **1.58 pips**.

These figures are historical observations, not guaranteed spreads for every OANDA entity.

### Core Pricing: Lower spreads plus commission

OANDA also offers Core Spreads + Commission pricing in certain jurisdictions.

For its US entity, the broker publishes a commission of:

- $0.70 per 10,000 currency units per side.
- $7 per 100,000 units per side.
- $14 for a complete round-turn trade of 100,000 units.

Core spreads can start from 0.0 pips on selected major pairs, but actual spreads vary.

According to [OANDA's official US Core Pricing terms](https://help.oanda.com/us/en/faqs/spreads-commission.htm), customers must deposit or maintain a balance of at least $10,000 to qualify.

The commission and requirements differ from those used by certain other OANDA entities.
