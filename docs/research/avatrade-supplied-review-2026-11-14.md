The broker explains this structure in its [official fees and charges guide](https://www.avatrade.com/trading-info/our-fees-charges).

### How does AvaProtect work?

Consider a hypothetical example.

A trader opens an eligible EUR/USD position and purchases AvaProtect coverage for a specified period.

If the position loses money during the protection period, AvaProtect may reimburse eligible trading losses according to the feature's terms.

If the position is profitable, the trader retains the eligible profit.

However, the protection premium is charged separately.
