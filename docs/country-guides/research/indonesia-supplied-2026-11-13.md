## Forex trading regulations in Indonesia

Forex trading in Indonesia is subject to a regulatory framework that distinguishes authorized domestic futures brokerage services from foreign platforms.

The **Badan Pengawas Perdagangan Berjangka Komoditi (Bappebti)** maintains an official register of futures brokers, known locally as *pialang berjangka*.

Indonesia also introduced changes to the supervision of financial derivatives beginning in January 2025. Certain financial derivatives linked to money-market and foreign-exchange instruments came under **Bank Indonesia**, while other categories of financial derivatives and digital financial assets moved to the **Financial Services Authority (OJK)**.

Bank Indonesia subsequently introduced additional rules for money-market and foreign-exchange derivatives effective December 1, 2025.

These institutional changes mean traders should verify which regulator and legal requirements apply to the actual product being offered.

### What Indonesian traders should check

| Regulatory consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Domestic broker licence** | Establishes whether the company appears in the relevant Indonesian authorization records |
| **Broker's legal entity** | Determines which company holds the account |
| **Foreign regulatory licence** | Shows oversight in another jurisdiction, not necessarily Indonesian authorization |
| **Product classification** | Spot currency exchange, retail forex derivatives, and other financial contracts may fall under different rules |
| **Client protections** | Margin treatment, complaints, and negative-balance rules differ |
| **Payment arrangements** | Permitted funding methods and currency-conversion terms must be confirmed |

- [Bappebti broker verification](https://ceklegalitas.bappebti.go.id/)

## Indonesian regulatory protections explained

Indonesia's financial regulatory framework provides oversight for different market activities and financial products.

| Area | What traders should verify |
|---|---|
| Broker licensing | Whether the firm appears in the appropriate Indonesian register |
| Financial-derivative oversight | Which authority supervises the exact product |
| Client funds | Whether applicable rules require segregation and how funds are held |
| Margin and risk disclosures | What the client must understand before leveraged trading |

These protections depend on the applicable regulations, company, and product.

A foreign licence should never be presented as equivalent to domestic authorization.

Investor compensation arrangements are not universal.

Before opening an account, confirm:

- The exact legal company holding the account
