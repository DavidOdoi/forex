## Forex trading rules in Pakistan

The SBP's May 18, 2022 circular, **EPD Circular Letter No. 08 of 2022**, addresses offshore forex, margin-trading, and CFD platforms.

It explains that purchases of services through the offshore platforms covered by the circular violate the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947, and that direct or indirect foreign-exchange remittances to these platforms are not permitted.

The circular specifically mentions platforms such as OctaFX and Easy Forex as examples.

The SECP issued further warnings about unauthorized online trading and investment platforms in April and December 2025.

These notices are relevant to Pakistani residents considering online trading services, especially when a provider advertises international regulation without Pakistani authorization.

### What the rules mean for retail traders

| Rule or consideration | What it means |
|---|---|
| **SBP offshore trading restrictions** | Residents must not assume that funding offshore forex or CFD accounts is permitted |
| **SECP licensing** | Verify a broker's authorization for the relevant regulated activity |
| **PMEX participation** | Provides access to exchange-listed futures through eligible registered brokers |
| **Contract availability** | A PMEX broker may not offer every currency or derivative product |
| **Foreign broker licences** | Overseas authorization does not override Pakistan's local restrictions |
| **Investor protection** | Legal rights and complaint procedures depend on the product and licensed entity |
