## Forex trading regulations in Ghana

Ghana's foreign-exchange and investment markets involve two important regulatory authorities:

- The **Bank of Ghana (BoG)** oversees banking, foreign-exchange operations, and the interbank foreign-exchange market.
- The **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC Ghana)** regulates securities-market activities and relevant investment services.

These authorities have different responsibilities.

Ordinary currency exchange, interbank FX brokerage, securities investment services, and leveraged retail forex or CFD trading are not necessarily governed by identical requirements.

### Bank of Ghana's 2026 FX-broker rules

Under Ghana's Interbank Forex Market Conduct Rules, local and international FX brokers seeking to operate in the country's **interbank foreign-exchange market** require prior Bank of Ghana approval at the beginning of each calendar year.

For January 1 through December 31, 2026, the Bank of Ghana authorized 12 interbank FX brokers, including:

- IC Securities
- SIC Brokers
- Serengeti Limited
- Sarpong Capital
- Terika Financial Services Limited
- Savvy Africa
- GFX Brokers
- Regulus
- MB&CO Capital Limited
- ICAP
- Obsidian Achernar
- TAW Impact Capital

These companies belong to Ghana's regulated interbank FX framework.

**Crucially, this is not a retail CFD-broker recommendation list.** Being authorized to operate in the interbank market does not automatically mean a company offers leveraged forex trading accounts to individual customers.

### SEC Ghana and online investment platforms

On June 23, 2026, SEC Ghana issued a directive concerning market operators, fintech service providers, and persons operating online investment and trading platforms.

The regulator highlighted the risks posed by unregistered platforms providing access to local and foreign securities markets.

On July 22, 2026, SEC Ghana also identified several entities operating without the required capital-market licences.

These developments make it important to verify whether a platform has the necessary authorization for the particular investment activity it offers.

### What Ghanaian traders should verify

| Regulatory consideration | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Bank of Ghana approval** | Relevant to activities governed by the country's foreign-exchange framework, including interbank FX brokerage |
| **SEC Ghana licensing** | Relevant to regulated securities and investment activities |
| **Foreign regulatory licences** | Show authorization in another jurisdiction, not necessarily Ghana |
| **Contracting company** | Identifies the legal entity responsible for the account |
| **Product eligibility** | Establishes which forex, CFD, or other products are offered |
| **Client protections** | Determines which rules and safeguards apply |
| **Funding arrangements** | Clarifies permitted payment routes and applicable charges |

Before selecting a financial provider, consult the official regulator and verify the exact service.

Useful sources include the [Bank of Ghana](https://www.bog.gov.gh/) and [SEC Ghana](https://sec.gov.gh/).

## Bank of Ghana and SEC protections explained

Ghana's financial regulators oversee different financial activities.

| Area | What it means for traders |
|---|---|
| **Bank of Ghana FX oversight** | Supervises relevant foreign-exchange activities and authorized interbank FX brokers |
| **SEC licensing** | Applies to regulated securities and investment activities |
| **Interbank broker authorization** | Requires annual approval for entities operating in Ghana's interbank FX market |
| **Regulatory warnings** | Help identify unauthorized investment businesses and schemes |
| **Client disclosures** | Explain contractual obligations, risks, and account terms where applicable |
| **Complaint channels** | Provide a route to report issues involving activities within the regulator's jurisdiction |
