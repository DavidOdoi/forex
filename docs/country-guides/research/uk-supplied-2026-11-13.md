## UK CFD rules for retail traders

The FCA’s [CFD rules](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences) apply to firms offering CFDs and CFD-like options to retail consumers in or from the UK. They require firms to limit leverage to between 30:1 and 2:1 depending on the underlying asset, close positions when funds fall to 50% of the required margin, and ensure a retail client cannot lose more than the funds in the trading account. [fca.org](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences)

For major forex pairs, the FCA requires retail clients to post margin of at least 3.33% of the trade’s exposure, equivalent to maximum leverage of 30:1. Minor forex pairs require at least 5% margin, equivalent to maximum leverage of 20:1. [handbook.fca.org](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5)

| Product / underlying | Minimum retail margin | Maximum retail leverage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Major forex pair | 3.33% | 30:1 |
| Minor forex pair | 5% | 20:1 |
| Major stock index | 5% | 20:1 |
| Gold | 5% | 20:1 |
| Other commodity | 10% | 10:1 |
| Shares and other assets | 20% | 5:1 |

These limits apply to retail clients. Professional clients may be able to access higher leverage, but they lose some retail protections. The FCA specifically warns consumers who “opt up” to professional status that they lose some protections otherwise available to retail customers. [fca.org](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences)

## FCA protections explained

The FCA’s retail CFD framework includes four major protections:

- **Leverage limits:** Maximum leverage ranges from 30:1 to 2:1 depending on the underlying asset. [fca.org](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences)
- **Margin close-out rule:** A firm must close a retail client’s position when net equity falls below 50% of the margin required to maintain open positions. [handbook.fca.org](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5)
- **Negative-balance protection:** A retail client’s liability is limited to the funds in the trading account. [handbook.fca.org](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5)
- **Risk warnings and no cash inducements:** Firms must provide a standardised risk warning showing the percentage of retail accounts that lose money, and may not offer cash or other inducements to encourage retail trading. [fca.org](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences)

These protections apply to retail clients. Professional clients may access different terms, but they lose some retail protections. [fca.org](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/contract-for-differences)

## FSCS protection: what it covers

FSCS protection is different from negative-balance protection.

FSCS may compensate eligible clients if an FCA- or PRA-authorised firm fails and cannot pay claims. For investment claims relating to firms that failed after 1 April 2019, the limit is up to £85,000 per eligible person, per firm. 

FSCS does **not** compensate for poor investment performance or trading losses. It may protect you where a regulated firm fails and there is a shortfall in money or assets it is holding for you, subject to eligibility rules. 

| Protection | What it covers | What it does not cover |
|---|---|---|
| **Negative-balance protection** | Limits a retail client’s liability to the funds in the CFD trading account.  [handbook.fca.org](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5) | Does not prevent losses within the account. |
| **FSCS investment protection** | May compensate eligible clients if an authorised firm fails and owes them a legal liability.  | Does not insure trading losses or poor investment performance. |
| **FSCS deposit protection** | May protect eligible deposits held with UK-authorised banks, building societies, and credit unions, up to £120,000 per eligible person.  | Is separate from investment-firm protection and does not apply simply because you trade CFDs. |

## Red flags to avoid

Avoid a broker or agent if you see any of the following:

- No clear legal company name in the account agreement
- Regulation claims that cannot be checked on the [FCA Register](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/?locale=en)
- Promises of guaranteed profits or “risk-free” trading
- Pressure to deposit quickly
- Requests to send money to a personal bank account
- Bonuses with unclear withdrawal restrictions
- No demo account or unclear withdrawal process
- Unrealistic leverage marketed without risk warnings

### Is FSCS protection the same as negative-balance protection?

No. Negative-balance protection limits a retail client’s liability to the funds in the CFD trading account. [FSCS](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/investments/) protection may compensate eligible clients if an authorised firm fails and owes them a legal liability; it does not insure trading losses. [handbook.fca.org](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5)
