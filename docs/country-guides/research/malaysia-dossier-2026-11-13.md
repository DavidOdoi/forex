**Practical conclusion:** For an ordinary Malaysian resident retail reader, a page should not present offshore forex/CFD brokers as locally authorised retail CFD providers. The SC framework expressly excludes retail investors from Malaysian CFDs, and the permitted Malaysian CFD underlyings are shares and equity indices—not forex. [sc.com](https://www.sc.com.my/api/documentms/download.ashx?id=d9cfb760-4acf-45bf-96de-a4281e86f991)

### Important distinction

Exness’s Labuan presence is a **geographic and corporate-presence fact**, not proof of Malaysian retail CFD permission. The SC’s rules require a distinct derivatives/CFD licence for a CFD provider, restrict Malaysian CFDs to sophisticated investors, and limit permitted underlyings to shares and equity indices. [sc.com](https://www.sc.com.my/api/documentms/download.ashx?id=d9cfb760-4acf-45bf-96de-a4281e86f991)

Likewise, XM’s Malaysia-localised website and “Trader from Malaysia” reference are **not** evidence that XM’s Cyprus entity is authorised, permitted, or contracting with Malaysian retail residents. [xm](https://www.xm.com/my/research-portal)
