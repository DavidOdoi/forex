## Editorial and accessibility checks

- Photographs identify the actual place; they do not imply local authorisation, an institution’s endorsement, or a funded trading test.
- India’s visible stock ticker is explicitly historical. No cover is presented as live prices.
- Cards use decorative empty alt attributes because the adjacent headline supplies the link name; article figures use descriptive, accurate alt text.
- The USDC logo identifies a token, not a network. The wallet photo contains no recovery phrase or account details and is not a product recommendation.
- Share-alike/source links and modification disclosures are visible in the credit gallery.
- Source selection included visual inspection: an incorrectly labelled Bank of Zambia candidate and Portuguese Lagos photographs were rejected.
