# Forex commission calculator — Batch 5, tool 18

Route: `/calculators/forex-commission-calculator`. Supporting article: 1,683 independently counted words, a 91-word introduction and exactly three FAQs. Uses the existing shared calculator page and the cost workspace introduced for spreads.

Fee modes: per billed lot per side; per billed lot round turn; per million base-currency units per side; and percentage of base/quote notional. Configured platform-lot units and billed-lot units are separate. Quote-notional percentage fees use each executed order's price and require the fee denomination to match the selected notional currency. Fees then use the shared liability-conversion resolver. A separate quote-to-account conversion can value pips when commission and pip denominations differ.

Custom ordered entry/exit rows support partial closes and reject closes exceeding previously opened volume. Minimums apply per charged executed order, not automatically per partial fill. Round-turn timing is either split equally or prepaid at entry; prepaid closing rows have no additional fee/minimum. Optional original-currency half-up order rounding is disclosed, and minimums incompatible with that precision are rejected. Already-charged fees affect remaining charges without changing the modeled total. Repetition totals do not recommend trading frequency.

No live broker schedule or verified broker pricing preset is connected. Tiered/capped fees, minimums per fill and undocumented broker rounding require separate checking. The official Interactive Brokers spot-currency pricing page was fetched and read: it documents tiered transaction-value fees, per-order minimums and conversion to the account base currency. It is an example of a fee schedule, not a recommendation or a claim that its terms apply to the user. Other contextual primary references cover MetaQuotes contract properties, MetaTrader executable bid/ask prices and Frankfurter dated reference conversions.
