For each broker answer:

1. **Identity and regulation:** What is its current official domain? Identify each relevant contracting legal entity, jurisdiction, regulator, licence number, current status and authorised activity. 2. **Country scope:** Which residents can or cannot open an account with each entity, according to current official terms? 3. **Account comparison:** For each account and entity, give account name, base currencies, minimum deposit, minimum trade size, advertised minimum spread versus typical EUR/USD spread if documented, commission currency and amount per standard lot per side or round trip, and applicable retail leverage. 4. **Other costs:** What documented overnight financing, swap-free eligibility/administration charges, inactivity, conversion, deposit and withdrawal fees apply? Explain exclusions and entity/account differences.
5. **Quick facts:** Is a demo available? Is mobile trading available and on which platform? Is a swap-free option offered? What does each entity say about segregated client money and negative-balance protection? Does it accept US residents? Supply evidence for each yes/no answer.
6. **Deposits and withdrawals:** List documented methods, currencies, minimums, charges, name-matching requirements and restrictions. Separate broker processing time from bank/payment arrival. Do not describe advertised times as tested or guaranteed. Do not assume mobile-money support across countries.
7. **Platforms and markets:** Identify current platforms, web/mobile availability and documented instrument classes. Note meaningful restrictions; date any instrument counts.
8. **Protections and warnings:** Explain the actual entity-specific compensation eligibility, complaints route and applicable risk warnings. Report relevant official enforcement or warnings using primary evidence and the exact legal entity. Never equate licensing or segregated funds with guaranteed safety.
9. **Customer feedback:** Find the correct Trustpilot profile; give its exact URL, displayed score, review count and access date, or say unavailable. Distinguish different domains/entities. Customer reviews are opinions, not verified events; a Trustpilot score is not our editorial rating.
10. **Useful editorial takeaways:** Provide 3–5 specific, evidence-backed pros and 3–5 cons where supported, who the service may suit, and three brief broker-specific FAQs. Avoid generic filler or unsupported 'best' claims. Do not invent experience, live-account testing, profitable outcomes or star ratings.
