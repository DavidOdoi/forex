## How to start forex trading in India: check the local route

RBI’s [Forex Transactions FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3347), cited as updated on 28 August 2024, says resident persons may undertake forex transactions only with authorised persons and for permitted purposes under FEMA. Electronic transactions must use the permitted authorised-platform or recognised-exchange route.

The same FAQ directly answers overseas margin remittances: margins or margin calls to overseas exchanges or counterparties are not allowed under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme. The [RBI’s LRS FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/English/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=1834) also lists remittances for trading foreign exchange abroad among prohibited purposes.

A general remittance limit does not override those exclusions. A broker accepting Indian customers, an offshore wallet or cryptocurrency payment option does not establish FEMA or LRS compliance. Do not use another payment method to treat a prohibited purpose as permitted.

The [NSE currency-derivatives overview](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/about-currency-derivatives) is an exchange reference for learning contract types. It does not mean every resident may use every product for every purpose. Verify current eligibility, exposure and purpose requirements with an authorised bank and the relevant exchange or provider.

## Check venue and purpose, not just the currency symbol

The same currency symbol can appear in different legal products. Establish the permitted entity, execution venue and purpose before analysing a chart. For INR-related contracts, ask specifically about applicable underlying-exposure requirements; do not treat an old pair list or a broker login as eligibility.

## What to prepare before opening an account

Prepare information, not a large deposit. Requirements depend on the lawful provider and product; check its current document list before sending sensitive records.

- A reliable device and internet connection, plus a secure email address.
- Government-issued identity and address evidence accepted by the provider, if required.
- Payment and contact details in your own name; no borrowed identity or third-party wallet.
- A written risk budget that excludes rent, food, family commitments and debt payments.

## Understand the product and its costs

Read the actual exchange contract

| Contract size | The exchange defines the quantity; do not assume an offshore FX lot. |

Use exchange educational material to learn order entry and settlement. Price exposure is a contract calculation, not simply the amount of cash initially paid. Do not open an offshore demo and mistake its trading rules for the rules of a permitted domestic product.

## Compare evidence before choosing a provider

Ask who issues the contract and handles your money, not just which app you will install. Match the account agreement, licence claim and official payment instructions. Keep screenshots and dated copies of the documents you relied on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does access to MT4 or MT5 establish permission in India?

No. An app does not establish the permitted entity, product, execution venue or purpose. Check current RBI and exchange requirements before using real funds.

### Can LRS fund an overseas forex margin account?

RBI’s Forex Transactions FAQ says remittances for margins or margin calls to overseas exchanges or counterparties are not allowed under LRS. A general remittance allowance does not override that restriction.

### Can I use a standard offshore forex lot in my calculation?

Use the actual permitted contract’s specifications. Exchange contract size, tick value, expiry and settlement can differ from an OTC forex product; a generic lot assumption can misstate exposure.
