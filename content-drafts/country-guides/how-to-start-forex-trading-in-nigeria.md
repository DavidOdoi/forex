## How to start forex trading in Nigeria: check the local route

The [CBN’s January 2025 Nigeria FX Code](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2025/CCD/Nigeria%20FX%20Code%20.pdf) identifies authorised dealers and other licensed wholesale-market participants. Its conduct rules are not a general permission for an individual to fund an overseas retail CFD account.

The [CBN’s May 2024 bureau guidelines](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2024/FPRD/APPROVED%20BDC%20GUIDELINES%20May%2022%202024.pdf) concern bureaux de change. Restrictions or permissions for those businesses must not be transplanted to an individual retail trading account.

Use the SEC’s [registered-operator search](https://sec.gov.ng/for-investors/find-a-registered-operator/) to investigate an investment-service claim, then verify the activity covered. Company incorporation, a payment processor and an overseas group licence are different checks.

We have not verified a blanket permission for Nigerian residents to fund overseas leveraged forex or CFD accounts. Describe the actual contract and transfer purpose to SEC, CBN and your bank. Do not treat a successful deposit as an answer to those regulatory questions.

## Separate a naira payment from regulatory permission

A local recipient or a payment processor may make a transfer look domestic while the trading contract is with an overseas company. Ask who receives the money, where the account is held and which company must repay a withdrawal. Keep all three answers together.

## What to prepare before opening an account

Prepare information, not a large deposit. Requirements depend on the lawful provider and product; check its current document list before sending sensitive records.

- A reliable device and internet connection, plus a secure email address.
- Government-issued identity and address evidence accepted by the provider, if required.
- Payment and contact details in your own name; no borrowed identity or third-party wallet.
- A written risk budget that excludes rent, food, family commitments and debt payments.

## Understand the product and its costs

A short forex glossary

| Term | What to understand before practising |
| --- | --- |
| Currency pair | The price of a base currency in a quote currency; EUR/USD is dollars per euro. |
| Pip and position size | For EUR/USD, a pip commonly means 0.0001; cash exposure depends on the units traded. |
| Spread and commission | The buy-sell price gap and any separate fees, potentially charged on both sides. |
| Leverage and margin | A small deposit can support a larger exposure; losses follow the exposure. |
| Financing | Holding a position overnight may add a charge or credit under the account terms. |
| Protective order | A stop is an instruction, not a guarantee of execution at that exact price. |

Hypothetical example: buying 1,000 euros at EUR/USD 1.1000 and closing at 1.1010 produces US$1 gross profit. The opposite ten-pip move produces US$1 gross loss. Costs and account-currency conversion change the net result. This is classroom arithmetic, not an available-product or trade recommendation.

## Compare evidence before choosing a provider

Ask who issues the contract and handles your money, not just which app you will install. Match the account agreement, licence claim and official payment instructions. Keep screenshots and dated copies of the documents you relied on.

Questions to ask before registering

| Area | Ask for |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and permission | Legal company, relevant activity, jurisdiction and current register entry or official confirmation. |
| Total trading costs | Typical spread, opening/closing commission, financing and conversion; do not compare minimum spreads alone. |
| Client treatment | Account classification, complaint process, custody and terms that actually apply to your entity. |
| Payments | Official recipient, accepted account holder, full charges and withdrawal conditions in writing. |

## Check funding and withdrawals separately

Ask your bank whether the proposed transfer purpose, destination and recipient are permitted. Do not assume a debit card transaction, peer-to-peer transfer or foreign-currency account changes the rules applying to the underlying margin account.

Request an illustration of the naira paid, foreign currency credited, intermediary charges and likely return conversion. A favourable trading result in dollars can be reduced by conversion costs when measured in naira.

Reject instructions to use another person’s account or disguise a payment purpose. Where a properly verified route is available, retain the bank advice, account agreement and withdrawal conditions.

Choose one simple practice setup and write the entry, exit and maximum loss before placing an order. Record whether you followed the plan. A profitable demo run does not establish that the strategy, execution or emotional response will be the same with real money.

## Set a cash loss budget before sizing

Illustrative budgeting only: on NGN 100,000, a 1% planned loss budget is NGN 1,000. This is a teaching example, not a suitable deposit recommendation or proof that a particular account is available. A stop can slip, so actual losses can exceed the plan.

Trading capital and a broker’s minimum deposit are different decisions. If the smallest permitted position already exceeds your cash limit after costs, remain in simulation. More leverage does not make that position safer.

Our [forex position-size calculator](/tools/forex-position-size-calculator) supports a USD teaching example and selected currencies, but not NGN accounts. Convert the actual pip value and costs correctly before using a local-currency loss budget.

> **Keep essential money outside the account** Never borrow trading capital or use money needed for living expenses. A small planned percentage is not a guarantee against loss, gaps, insolvency or fraud.

## Avoid these country-specific shortcuts

- Treating company incorporation as permission to offer a leveraged product.
- Assuming a local payment processor is the trading counterparty.
- Ignoring the cost of converting naira twice.
- Using an old regulatory claim without checking its current status.

Keep trade, fee, conversion and payment records. Ask a qualified local tax adviser how your actual instruments and circumstances are treated; this guide supplies no universal tax rate or exemption.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a foreign broker licence establish Nigerian approval?

It does not establish Nigerian permission for your account. Verify the exact product, legal entity, applicable SEC requirements and lawful funding route before depositing.

### Can I fund a broker from my naira account?

Ask your bank about the specific purpose and recipient, and verify the provider separately. A successful payment or available card option does not prove that the underlying trading arrangement is permitted.

### Should I choose a broker mainly for a low deposit?

No. Establish the lawful route, identity and full costs first. Then compare the minimum trade size with an affordable loss budget; a low deposit does not control leverage risk.
